The ways to Santiago

Which Camino is right for you?

Every route ends at the same cathedral. They just ask something different of you along the way. The distances are the classic full routes, and you can always walk a shorter stretch.

about 770 km The classic Camino Francés Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port · 31 to 35 days The classic route, and the one most people mean when they say the Camino.
about 610 km Camino Portugués Lisbon or Porto · 25 to 28 days from Lisbon Gentler climbs, coastal variants and a milder introduction to the Camino.
about 830 km Camino del Norte Irún · 32 to 36 days The coastal route along the Bay of Biscay. Quieter, harder, greener.
about 320 km Camino Primitivo Oviedo · 12 to 14 days The original route. Mountainous, wild and deeply rewarding.
about 115 km Camino Inglés Ferrol · 5 to 6 days The short one. A whole Camino feeling in a single week.
about 980 km Vía de la Plata Seville · 38 to 42 days The long, lonely trek from south to north for experienced walkers.
about 90 km Camino Finisterre Santiago de Compostela · 3 to 4 days The continuation to the end of the world, where pilgrims of old walked out to the sea.

About

The way is better when you share it

Camino King started as travel notes from my first Camino in 2016 and grew into the place I wish I had found before my own first step: clear routes, honest gear and stories that get you out the door.

It was never only about the kilometres. It is about the people you meet along the way and the feeling of standing on a ridge with the whole day laid out in front of you. Everything here has been walked, worn or weighed before it gets recommended.
