Food on the trail

Food gives a walking day meaning. Simple recipes for the campfire, the trangia and the kitchen at home, so you eat well on the trail.

Guides in food

Food

Eating before the Camino: how to fuel your training

The weeks before you leave matter more for your legs than the last long training walk. Here is what goes on the plate, and how to fit it in.
Food Banana pancakes you can fry over a campfire or Trangia Delicious banana pancakes made with banana, egg and oats. Easy energy food you can fry over a fire or on a Trangia and take along the way. Food Campfire goulash for four, simmered low while you make camp Heartwarming goulash with beef, paprika and root vegetables. It simmers tender over a fire or Trangia and fills you up after a long day on the trail. Food Chili con carne that looks after itself over the fire Rich chili con carne with beef, beans, corn and spices. A filling pot with warmth and bite, perfect for an evening around the fire. Food Homemade pizza baked over the fire or in the oven Pizza from scratch with a crisp base, homemade tomato sauce and fresh toppings. Bake it in the oven or over the fire after a day in the open. Food Pickled red onions, ready in an hour and better the next day Crisp, tangy and sweet pickled red onions that lift any dish. Easy to make at home and bring along as a colourful kick for your trail food. Food Pizza dough from scratch, the base for the trail pizza A soft, airy pizza dough with only a handful of ingredients. Knead it, let it rise, and you have the base for a genuinely good pizza, at home or on the trail. Food Korv stroganoff: Swedish sausage stew over a fire Korv stroganoff, the Swedish classic with sausages in a creamy paprika sauce. Rich and easy comfort food that also works on a camping stove. Food Chestnuts foraged and boiled on the Camino trail Healthy, tasty chestnuts cooked on the trail. All you need is chestnuts, water, salt and a pot. A cheap meal and a fine snack for the trip.
