Plan your first Camino

Ten steps from idea to departure, in the order they make sense. Each step has a guide that goes deeper.

1 Choose your route 2 Choose your season 3 Set a budget 4 Get your pilgrim passport 5 Book the journey and the first nights 6 Get in shape 7 Pack light 8 Look after feet and health 9 Get your phone ready 10 Learn the key words
  1. 1

    Choose your route

    Start with how much time you have. A week is enough for the Camino Inglés or the last 100 kilometres from Sarria, two weeks for the Camino Portugués from Porto, and a month for the whole Camino Francés.

    • The routes side by side
    • Which route to choose
    • Where to start the Camino Francés
    • The last 100 km from Sarria
    • How long the Camino is
  2. 2

    Choose your season

    May, June and September give the best balance between weather, open albergues and room on the trail. High summer is hot, and in winter a number of albergues are closed.

    • The Camino month by month
    • When to go, in short
  3. 3

    Set a budget

    Work out the daily cost of a bed, food and small things, and add the journey there and back. Then you know how many days the money covers.

    • What the Camino costs
    • Food costs on the Camino
  4. 4

    Get your pilgrim passport

    The pilgrim passport gives access to the albergues and is the proof when you want your Compostela in Santiago. You can get it before you leave or in your start town.

    • The pilgrim passport, in short
    • Arriving in Santiago
  5. 5

    Book the journey and the first nights

    Find your way to the start town and have a bed ready for the first night. The rest you can take day by day if you walk outside the busiest weeks.

    • Getting to the start town
    • Getting there, in short
    • Luggage transfer
  6. 6

    Get in shape

    The best thing you can do at home is walk. Long walks in the shoes you plan to wear, with the backpack packed.

    • How to prepare
    • Body and fitness
  7. 7

    Pack light

    Aim for a backpack of at most 10 percent of your body weight. The two most important choices are the backpack and what you wear on your feet.

    • The Camino packing list
    • Choosing a backpack
    • Shoes for the Camino
    • Hiking socks
  8. 8

    Look after feet and health

    Know how to prevent blisters, and sort out insurance and your health card before you travel.

    • Walking the Camino alone
    • Insurance and safety
    • Bed bugs on the Camino
  9. 9

    Get your phone ready

    Download maps and tickets so they work offline, and bring a way to keep the phone charged on long days.

    • Power bank for hiking
  10. 10

    Learn the key words

    A few Spanish phrases open doors, and the pilgrim words come up all the way. Look them up before you stand at the first albergue.

    • Spanish phrasebook
    • Camino glossary
